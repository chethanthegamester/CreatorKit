What is an Image Compressor?

An image compressor reduces the amount of data needed to store an image. Smaller image files can be easier to upload, share and store, and can help reduce the amount of data a website needs to load.

CreatorKit's image compressor lets you choose a quality level and process the image directly in your browser.

How to Compress an Image

1. Choose an image

Select a JPG, PNG, WebP or another image format supported by your browser.

2. Choose compression quality

Use the quality slider to choose the balance between file size and visual quality.

3. Compress the image

Click the compression button and CreatorKit will process the image in your browser.

4. Download your image

Check the resulting file size and download the compressed image if the result looks good.

Why Compress Images?

  • Reduce file sizes before uploading images.
  • Make images easier to share online.
  • Save storage space.
  • Prepare images for websites.
  • Reduce the size of images used in projects.
  • Make large photos easier to upload.

Image Compression Tips

Higher quality generally produces a larger file, while lower quality can produce a smaller file with more visible compression. Try different settings to find the balance that works for your particular image.

If you need to change the pixel dimensions of an image, use the CreatorKit Image Resizer instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. CreatorKit's image compressor is free to use.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The compression process happens directly in your browser.

Does compression reduce image quality?

Depending on the selected quality level, compression can reduce visual quality. Higher quality settings generally preserve more detail.

Can I compress JPG and PNG images?

Yes. The tool accepts common browser-supported image formats. The compressed output is generated as a JPG file.

Can compression make an image smaller than the original?

Often yes, although the exact reduction depends on the original image, its format, dimensions and selected quality.
