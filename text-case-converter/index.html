What is a Text Case Converter?

A text case converter changes the capitalization of text without requiring you to manually edit every word. It can be useful for documents, social media posts, YouTube descriptions, titles, notes, captions and many other types of writing.

CreatorKit's free Text Case Converter works directly in your browser, making it quick to transform text whenever you need it.

Text Case Options

UPPERCASE

Converts every letter to uppercase. Example: "hello world" becomes "HELLO WORLD".

lowercase

Converts every letter to lowercase. Example: "HELLO WORLD" becomes "hello world".

Title Case

Capitalizes the first letter of each word. Example: "this is my video" becomes "This Is My Video".

Sentence Case

Capitalizes the first letter of each sentence while making the remaining letters lowercase.

Capitalize Words

Capitalizes the first letter of each word while preserving the rest of each word.
