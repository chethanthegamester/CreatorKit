Hashtag Tips for Creators

Use hashtags that are actually related to your content. Avoid filling your posts with unrelated popular hashtags simply because they are trending.

A smaller collection of relevant hashtags can be more useful than a huge list of unrelated ones. Always check the current rules and best practices of the platform you're posting on.
