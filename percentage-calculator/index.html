What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word percentage literally refers to a value "per hundred."

Percentages are commonly used for discounts, statistics, grades, financial calculations, growth rates and many other everyday calculations.

Percentage Formulas

Percentage of a number = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number
What percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
Percentage change = ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100

Examples

What is 20% of 500?

20 ÷ 100 × 500 = 100

25 is what percentage of 100?

25 ÷ 100 × 100 = 25%

What is the percentage increase from 100 to 125?

((125 − 100) ÷ 100) × 100 = 25%
